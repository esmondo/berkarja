# Validation Criteria

**Dimensions**: Audience Relevance (0.25), Uniqueness (0.20), Feasibility (0.20), Brand Alignment (0.20), Impact (0.15)
**Scoring**: 1–5 (Reject to Excellent). Thresholds: passing 3.0, recommended 4.0
**Platform Fit**: TikTok (hook, trend-ready), IG (visual, 15–90s), YT (depth, retention)

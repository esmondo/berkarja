# Platform Algorithms 2026

Reference for how major platforms rank and distribute content in 2026.

## Instagram

- Reels prioritized in Explore and Feed
- Engagement velocity matters for reach
- Authentic, native formats favored

## YouTube

- Watch time and retention drive recommendations
- Shorts and long-form have separate funnels
- Community signals (comments, shares) weighted

## LinkedIn

- Dwell time and meaningful engagement (comments > likes)
- Creator mode boosts organic reach
- Newsletter integration affects distribution

## TikTok

- Completion rate and loop potential
- Sound and trend adoption
- FYP personalization depth

## Twitter/X

- Reply and quote engagement
- Community Notes for fact-checking
- Paid vs organic mix considerations
